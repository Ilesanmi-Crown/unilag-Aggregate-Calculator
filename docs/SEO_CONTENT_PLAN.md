## 4. Policy on Programmatic / Department Pages

**Strict Decision: Do NOT create 112 individual programmatic pages for each department (e.g. `/cut-off/medicine`, `/cut-off/law`).**

### Rationale:
1. **Google Helpful Content Guidance:** Programmatic generation of 100+ pages containing only 1 number and boilerplate text represents "thin, near-duplicate content" that triggers quality devaluation across the entire domain.
2. **Superior User Experience:** Prospective candidates frequently compare cut-offs across related disciplines (e.g., Computer Engineering vs Systems Engineering vs Computer Science). Our single-page interactive directory ([`/` with live search and filter](file:///c:/Users/CROWN/Desktop/unilag_agreeate_calculator/src/components/DepartmentDirectorySection.tsx)) allows instant side-by-side comparison without navigating through 10 separate pages.
3. **Crawl Budget Optimization:** Consolidates all authority onto high-value, comprehensive guides rather than diluting PageRank across dozens of low-word-count programmatic URLs.
